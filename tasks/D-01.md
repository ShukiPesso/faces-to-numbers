## Goal
A short principles document exists that every design and UI task follows.

## Context
This is a memorial companion used on site, sometimes in a moving car, by people standing where others were
murdered. Read the three content files completely before writing — design for that material, not for a
generic tourism app.

- docs/03_CONVENTIONS.md (always)

## Owns files — you may create or modify ONLY these
- `design/01-principles.md`
- `reports/D-01.md` (your report)

## Steps
1. Read the brief and the three content files in full.
2. Write design/01-principles.md with these sections: Purpose (2 sentences); Emotional tone (5 adjectives we ARE, 5 we are NOT); 6–8 principles, each a one-line rule plus a do/don't example tied to a real screen of this app (home, station, person, drive segment, silence moment); Pacing and silence; Typography attitude; Color attitude (exactly when memorial red is allowed); Motion rules (≤ 200 ms, reduced motion); Imagery rules (no photos until cleared, anemone mark usage); Words (pointer to Conventions §1.8).
3. End with a 'Red flags' checklist of 10 items that a reviewer can use to reject a screen.

## Do NOT
- Do not propose gamification, badges, celebrations, dramatic dark themes or illustrations of the events.
- Do not change scope or content.

## Acceptance criteria — all must be true
- [ ] design/01-principles.md has every section listed and the 10-item red-flags checklist.
- [ ] Every principle has a concrete do/don't example tied to a named screen.
- [ ] The document is at most about two printed pages.

## How to verify
- Orchestrator reads it against docs/05_DESIGN_BRIEF.md §3 and the red flags.

## Definition of Done
Conventions §10 applies. Write `reports/D-01.md` from `templates/REPORT_TEMPLATE.md`. If anything is unclear or missing: stop, set status BLOCKED in the report, and ask (Conventions §11).
