## Goal
For people marked graphic, nothing the content warning hides is visible before the visitor chooses to see it: the regular opening moves behind the gate, and an optional gentle opening (written and approved by Shuki) is shown instead.

gentle opening paragraph without the hard detail. Shuki approves its wording (task C-07 drafts it). Until the
approved text exists in content-source, a graphic person shows NO opening outside the gate — only name,
subtitle, photo strip and the gate. Never generate or paraphrase text in code.
