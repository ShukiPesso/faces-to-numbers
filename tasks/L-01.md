Shuki gets one page that says, with evidence, whether the site is ready for its first real visitors — and nothing goes public with an unverified quote or without the families' answer.

## Steps
1. Collect the verdicts: T-11 test audit (READY?), D-09 design QA, C-04 rendered proofread, H-05 field test, H-06 sign-off, H-03 consent tracker, Lighthouse from T-09, last deploy + smoke from O-03.
2. Content gate: in content-source/VERIFICATION.md count items still not ✅. Any quote or fact not verified → NO-GO unless Shuki explicitly removed it from the site edition (check CORRECTIONS.md).
3. Family gate: for every person, record the consent status from H-03. A family that declined → their page must not be live (check the content). Photos only for status granted (H-04 is after launch — at launch all photos are placeholders).
4. Safety gate: field test confirmed that the driver warning, offline mode and location button work in the car on the real route.
5. Write review/L-01-launch.md: a GO / NO-GO table with evidence links, open risks from docs/08_RISKS.md with their current state, and the soft-launch plan — first link shared only with Shuki's own team and 2–3 trusted guides, a short feedback form (a mailto link to contactEmail is enough), a review date two weeks later.
6. Do not announce publicly. Shuki decides when to share wider.

## Do NOT
- Do not mark GO if any HIGH finding in review/ is open.
- Do not post, email or share the URL anywhere yourself.

## Acceptance criteria — all must be true
