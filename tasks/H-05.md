## Steps

2. At every segment check: is 'איפה אני' correct? Are the access notes still true (Tkuma hours, Black Arrow, parking)? Is the text readable in the sun? Does read-aloud work for the passenger? Is the duration realistic?
3. Record every issue in review/H-05-field-test.md: segment, phone, screenshot, description. Record the actual time per segment.
