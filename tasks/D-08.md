## Steps
1. Design a simple single-color mark: an abstract anemone (כלנית), or a road line with a small anemone, on the paper background. SVG that still reads at 16 px.
2. Export: design/brand/mark.svg, favicon.svg, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, icon-maskable-512.png (content within the central 80% safe zone), apple-touch-icon-180.png, og-image-1200x630.png (mark + 'פנים למספרים' + the app tagline from contracts/strings.he.ts, on paper background).
3. design/brand/README.md: usage rules and the exact file list with pixel sizes.
