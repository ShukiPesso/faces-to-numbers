# H-03 — Inform the families and record their consent (runs in parallel; blocks public launch)

| Agent | Model | Wave | Size | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| human | human | 1 · Parallel lanes start | L | todo |

**Depends on:** none — can start immediately  
**Unblocks:** H-04, L-01

## Goal
Each of the nine families has been told about the project, has seen the text about their loved one, and their response is recorded.

## Context
This runs alongside the whole build and blocks only the PUBLIC launch (L-01). It is the most important protection
for the families and for the project. A request to change or remove a story is a valid and respected outcome.

## Read-only inputs
- docs/03_CONVENTIONS.md (always)
- `content-source/people/`
- `docs/07_DECISIONS_FOR_SHUKI.md`

## Owns files — you may create or modify ONLY these
- `review/H-03-consent-tracker.md`
- `reports/H-03.md` (your report)

## Steps
1. Create review/H-03-consent-tracker.md with one row per person: how the family can be reached (organization, memorial page, mutual acquaintance), date contacted, response, requested changes, photo permission (yes/no, which photo, exact credit text).
2. Personalize the letter in the appendix and attach that person's text (print of content-source/people/NN_<id>.md or of the person page).
3. Pass every requested text change to the orchestrator as a correction to apply (it is logged in content-source/CORRECTIONS.md).
4. Follow up at most twice.

## Do NOT
- Do not publish publicly before every family has responded or had a reasonable attempt.
- Do not pressure anyone.

## Acceptance criteria — all must be true
- [ ] The tracker has 9 rows with dates and outcomes; photo permissions include credit text.

## How to verify
- Orchestrator reviews the tracker before L-01.
