## Steps
1. Build expectations from the real content JSON (read with fs in the spec or via helpers/routes.ts) — never hard-code names or counts.
2. E2E-NAV-01: '/' → click start-tour → '/tour' → open the first station → loop: assert h1 equals the station title and document.title contains it; for each primary person of the station open person-card-<id> and assert the person h1 equals the name, go back; click next-station until there is none; the last page is the last station. Use getByTestId and getByRole only.
3. E2E-NAV-02: page.goto('/#/station/<slug>') for every station and '/#/person/<id>' for every person → correct h1 without visiting '/' first. '/#/station/nope' and '/#/person/nope' → NotFound with a link that goes to '/'.
4. E2E-NAV-03: open the third station, click mark-visited, reload; go to '/' → resume-tour is visible and goes to the fourth station; click reset-progress → resume-tour disappears.
