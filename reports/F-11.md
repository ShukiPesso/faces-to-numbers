## Questions / risks / things I noticed outside my scope
1. Three texts in the designed SVG have no `he.ts` key and I may not add keys (§2.5), so I omitted them: "גבול רצועת עזה", "צפון" (the arrow and dashed border line are kept) and the in-SVG "תרשים סכמטי, לא מפה ולא בקנה מידה". The visible caption (`tour.mapAlt`, "מפה סכמטית…") partly covers the last. Suggest adding three keys and restoring them.
2. The legend uses `tour.kind.drive` / `tour.kind.stop` for "נסיעה" / "עצירה".
3. Marker ids are fixed to the real station ids (sderot, route-232, …). A station not in the list is skipped; the e2e fictional fixtures (test-a…) would show an empty map.
4. `route-map.md` defines states by colour values, not class names; I used `is-visited` / `is-current` on the marker group plus token-backed Tailwind classes. tour.md lists only primary/ink/muted/surface tokens, route-map.md also gold; I followed route-map.md (gold) as it is the D-05 spec.
5. Marker links are `tabindex=-1` / `aria-hidden` (pointer-only), per tour.md; hit area is an invisible 44-unit circle.
6. `title` tooltips on visited markers use "{title} — ביקרתם כאן".
