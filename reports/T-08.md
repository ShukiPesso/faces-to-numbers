## Violations found (real, not fixed)
- Rule: `nested-interactive` (serious), route `/tour`, both mobile-chrome and desktop.
- Selector: `.h-auto` (the `<svg role="img" data-testid="route-map" class="block h-auto w-full font-body">`).
- Cause: the svg has `role="img"` but contains focusable `<Link>` markers (`tabIndex={-1}`, `aria-hidden="true"`); axe still counts them as focusable descendants of an image role.
- Help: https://dequeuniversity.com/rules/axe/4.10/nested-interactive
- Owner: F-11 / F-11b, `src/components/tour/RouteMap.tsx` (Marker component). Possible fix for owner: render markers non-focusable inside the svg (e.g. no `<a>` there, with the station list as the real navigation), or use a different role on the svg.
