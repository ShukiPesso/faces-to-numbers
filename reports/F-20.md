## Questions / risks / things I noticed outside my scope
1. The font-family stacks live in `tokens.css` (not mine). They already list `'Heebo'` after `'Assistant'` and `'David Libre'` after `'Frank Ruhl Libre'`, and neither is bundled, so I gave the adjusted fallback faces those two family names. A cleaner fix would be a dedicated name (e.g. `'Assistant Fallback'`) added to the stacks in `tokens.css`. A visitor with Heebo or David Libre installed locally would get the fallback face instead of their copy (negligible).
2. Fallbacks use Arial / Times New Roman only (metrics computed from them). Android devices without Arial fall through to the next stack entry unadjusted; I did not use Noto/David because their metrics differ and cannot be matched by one rule.
3. Remaining CLS 0.024 on the person page is probably residual font or content settling; not investigated further, within target.
4. I did not eyeball the panel in a screenshot at 360 px or lg; behaviour is covered by CSS tokens and existing tests only. The design-QA agent should check the panel position with and without the sticky bar, and the lg SideNav offset.
5. `design/components/layout.md` §6 still describes the indicator as in-flow in the banner slot; the doc needs updating by its owner.
