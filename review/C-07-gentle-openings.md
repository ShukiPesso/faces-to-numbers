# C-07 — Gentle openings for the five graphic people (drafts for Shuki)

**Status:** DRAFT — nothing here is approved. Shuki approves or edits; then the orchestrator adds a
`## פתיחה עדינה · gentleOpening` section to each person file (content-source/README.md, person sections).
**Date:** 2026-10-12
**Why:** design QA D-09, finding #3 — on graphic people's pages the `ContentWarningGate` hides `oct7` and
the quotes, but the short `opening` shown above it repeats the hard detail.
**Graphic people (front matter `oct7Sensitivity: graphic`):** 03 ניצן ליבשטיין · 05 ענר שפירא ·
06 הירש גולדברג-פולין · 07 רז פרץ · 09 אביאל רחמים.

**Method (rules I followed):**
- Every word comes from the same person's own file in `content-source/people/` (current text, after the
  corrections in `CORRECTIONS.md`). Only cuts: whole sentences or clauses dropped. Nothing reworded, no new
  words, no new adjectives. Punctuation kept as in the source; where two pieces are joined, the join is
  marked in the notes.
- Left out: every injury, wound, weapon, grenade/shot, manner of death, finding of a body, and any number
  tied to the violence — everything the gate hides.
- Kept (deliberately): facts that are already visible outside the gate today — the subtitle, `personality`,
  `past`, `after`. Shuki should decide whether words like ״נרצח בשבי״ or ״במחיר חייו״ belong in a gentle
  opening (see the note for each person).
- The opening is also shown on the station's person card, so I avoided "here / this" phrasing (״המיגונית
  הקטנה הזו״) unless noted.
